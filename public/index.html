      What is Gravle?
      
    
      Gravle is a free daily physics game. Every day there are five new experiments: guess where a marble will land, which object wins a race, whether a tower stands, where a laser beam ends, how long a bridge must be, which objects float, how gears turn and more. Make your prediction, release, and watch the simulation. You get two attempts a day and your best one counts on the global leaderboard.
